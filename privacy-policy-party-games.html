Privacy Policy

Learn how information may be handled when you use this mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how Party Games (the "App") handles information when you use it. The App is a mobile game application. Its games, features, and content may be added, changed, or removed in future versions.

At a glance: The App does not require you to create an account. The App may request the Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission to send notifications, where supported and enabled. The App also uses Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase Analytics to understand app usage. These third-party SDKs may automatically collect or process certain technical, advertising, and usage information.

1. Information We Collect or Process

We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, videos, or precise location to use the App, unless a future feature clearly explains otherwise.

Information may be collected or processed through the App and its service providers, including:

  • App usage information: App launches, screen views, session activity, feature interactions, and other events configured in Firebase Analytics.
  • Device and technical information: Device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, app instance or device identifiers, diagnostic information, and approximate region that may be inferred from an IP address.
  • Advertising information: Advertising identifiers where available, ad impressions, ad interactions, information used for ad delivery and measurement, frequency management, and fraud prevention.
  • Notification information: Your notification permission setting and information needed to deliver notifications, if you grant permission.
  • Locally stored information: The App may store game progress, preferences, or settings on your device. Whether this information is stored locally depends on the features in the version you use.

The exact information processed depends on the App's features, SDK versions, SDK configuration, your device, your region, and your privacy choices.

2. Permissions

The App may request the Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission, where required by your Android version, to deliver notifications. This permission is optional unless a particular notification feature requires it. If you deny or revoke it, core game features should remain available, but notifications may not be delivered.

The App itself is not designed to request access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos, videos, or precise location for ordinary gameplay. Third-party SDKs included in the final build may declare or use additional permissions or APIs. The permissions in the released app are determined by the final merged Android manifest and SDK configuration; please review that manifest before publication.

3. Third-Party Services

The App currently uses the following third-party services. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display, deliver, measure, and improve advertisements. Depending on SDK version, configuration, region, and consent status, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to understand how users interact with the App and to help improve its features and performance. Depending on configuration, Firebase Analytics may automatically collect app events, session information, app instance identifiers, and device information. See Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

If additional SDKs, advertising mediation partners, analytics tools, or other third-party services are added in a future version, this Policy and the App's store disclosures should be updated to reflect those services.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its games;
  • Understand app usage and improve gameplay, usability, and performance;
  • Display and measure advertisements, manage ad frequency, and help prevent invalid activity or advertising fraud;
  • Diagnose technical issues and maintain the security of the App;
  • Send notifications when you have granted the relevant permission; and
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Advertising, Consent, and Privacy Choices

Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your location, applicable law, consent choices, and advertising configuration. Where required, a consent or privacy choices interface may be presented. You can also review advertising controls in your device or Google account settings.

For information about Google's advertising controls, visit Google My Ad Center. Available controls may vary by region and device.

6. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information directly to third parties. Information may be shared with or processed by service providers such as Google AdMob and Firebase for the purposes described in this Policy. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transaction, where permitted by applicable law.

7. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

Information stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by you, removed through device or App controls, or otherwise cleared. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to their settings, retention periods, and policies. We do not control retention periods independently applied by those providers.

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the App. Information transmitted by Google services is subject to the security measures described by Google. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The App's target age group and audience settings should be accurately declared in the relevant app store. If the App is directed to children or a mixed audience that includes children, additional legal and platform requirements may apply to advertising, analytics, consent, and data collection.

Parents or legal guardians who believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the App may contact us using the email below. We will review the request and take appropriate steps as required by applicable law.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Controls

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. To submit a request, contact us at the email address below. We may need to verify your request before responding.

You can manage notification permission through your Android settings. You may also reset or limit the use of your device's advertising identifier through the privacy or advertising settings available on your device. These controls may not stop all contextual advertising, analytics, or technical processing.

10. International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, those providers are responsible for using legally recognized safeguards for international transfers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy when the App, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
